          01
          
Payroll that HR can trust

          
The problem. Salaries depend on attendance, leave, shifts and loss-of-pay rules, and those rules change. A wrong slip costs real money and trust.

          
What I built. A payroll engine with configurable loss-of-pay rules, pay cycle periods, a whole-rupee day rate and statutory details on the salary sheet, plus a Testing Mode run so HR can check every salary before the real one.

        

        

          02
          
Accounts without typing twice

          
The problem. Purchase and payroll entries had to be keyed into Tally by hand after they were already in the ERP.

          
What I built. A Tally sync for purchase orders, goods receipts, payments and payroll, so accounts stay in step with operations.

        

        

          03
          
Attendance straight from the device

          
The problem. Attendance lived on biometric machines at the plant, away from payroll.

          
What I built. An eSSL sync that pulls punches into shifts and attendance, which payroll then reads directly.

        

        

          04
          
Rules that change without a developer

          
The problem. Who approves what changes often, and hard-coded approvers break the day someone moves roles.

          
What I built. Approval chains and settings that admins edit themselves, with role-based permissions on every screen and API.

        

        

          05
          
Bringing the old data across

          
The problem. Years of vendors and items sat in the previous procurement system, in messy formats.

          
What I built. Re-runnable import commands for vendors and the item master that clean the data as they load it, so imports can be repeated safely.

        

        

          06
          
Changing a live system safely

          
The problem. People use the ERP every day, so a broken release stops real work.

          
What I built. 97 automated tests across payments, quality checks, stock batches and vendor credit, self-test scripts for HR flows, and a full database backup before any risky change.
